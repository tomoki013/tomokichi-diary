# URLs are data, independent of slugs and ids

## Context

The previous site derived `/posts/<slug>` from the filename, so renaming a file moved a published URL.

## Decision

A `routes` table owns `path`, target, canonicality and redirects. Nothing derives a URL from a slug at runtime, and pages are generated by iterating that table.

## Why

URLs are the most valuable and least replaceable asset the site has; slugs are an editorial convenience. Separating them lets a slug be corrected without touching a URL, and makes a URL change an explicit, reviewable data change that carries its redirect with it.

## Consequences

Creating an article requires choosing its path. CI fails if a route has no page or a page has no route.
